Challenge #57

The Mummy

Hello detective! Kevin, the descendant of Arsène Lupin, has struck again. This time, he reportedly stole an ancient Egyptian tablet from the Louvre Museum. A witness claims to have heard the suspect say he was storing photos of his loot on MountainCloud Hosting servers while waiting for investigations to die down before selling them on the black market. With any luck, the photos will allow us to locate the place where he stores the stolen items. Access his MountainCloud account to find out.

Flag format

The flag is a postal address. It will have the following format:
XX_rue_xxxxxxxx_xxxxxx_ccccc_vvvvv-vvvvv
Examples:
32_rue_emile_zola_93200_saint-denis
29_rue_louis_blance_83990_saint-tropez
